Policies

Straight answers

Will the AI make up accomplishments?

No. Folioform can rephrase and organize your experience, but it never fabricates credentials, results or achievements.

Can I buy an add-on without a package?

No — add-ons require an eligible baseline package (Instant, Guided, Plus or Pro).

How are revisions counted?

Each submitted revision request uses one credit. Failed generations don't consume credits.

How long is a one-time portfolio hosted?

One-time packages include a 90-day hosted preview with analytics, then continued access through your downloaded static portfolio. Subscribers get hosting for as long as they're subscribed.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You keep any files you've exported. Ongoing hosting and editing access end according to the subscription terms.

Are my documents private?

Yes. Uploaded resumes and reports are private by default, and you can delete your documents and account at any time.
